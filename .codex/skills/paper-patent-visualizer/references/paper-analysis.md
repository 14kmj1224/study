# Academic paper analysis mode

## Required analysis

1. Identify the research question, hypothesis or objective, authors, venue, and publication date from the paper.
2. Describe samples, apparatus, controls, independent and dependent variables, procedure, and analysis method.
3. Extract quantitative results with units, uncertainty, sample size, and statistical treatment when reported.
4. Separate observed results from the authors' explanation and from the analyst's interpretation.
5. Check whether the conclusion is supported by the reported controls and data.
6. Record limitations, transferability constraints, missing controls, and questions for replication.

## Evidence map fields

- Finding
- Figure, table, or page
- Measurement or qualitative observation
- Conditions
- Uncertainty or limitation
- Interpretation status

Do not generalize beyond the tested materials, process window, equipment configuration, or sample population.
